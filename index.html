Forms Bot Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. About this service

iGroup Wealth operates the iGroup Wealth WhatsApp Forms Bot to help authorised users request blank business and servicing forms. This notice explains how personal information is handled in connection with this service.

The bot provides administrative assistance and does not provide personalised financial advice.

Please do not send completed forms, identity documents, banking details, medical information or confidential client information to the bot. Request the appropriate secure submission channel from iGroup Wealth.

2. Information we process

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, where supplied.
  • Messages, menu selections and requests you send to the bot.
  • Technical information, such as message identifiers, timestamps and delivery status.
  • Approved phone numbers used to control access to forms.
  • Information you provide when contacting us about privacy or requesting assistance.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Check whether a user is authorised to access forms.
  • Respond to messages and deliver requested blank forms.
  • Maintain the conversation's menu position.
  • Investigate errors, prevent misuse and protect the service.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

We do not sell information collected through the bot or use it to build advertising audiences.

4. Service providers and sharing

The bot uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to send and receive messages. Hosting and connectivity providers, including Cloudflare where used, may process information necessary to operate and secure the service.

Access within iGroup Wealth is limited to people who need it to administer or support the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Providers may process information outside South Africa. Their handling of information is also subject to their applicable privacy notices and service terms.

5. Storage and retention

Information is retained for as long as needed to operate the service, manage authorised access, resolve support issues and meet applicable legal obligations.

The current bot keeps conversation state and duplicate-message tracking in temporary server memory, which clears when the application restarts. Approved-number records and diagnostic logs are managed separately and may remain after a restart.

Restarting the bot does not delete WhatsApp chat histories, downloaded files or records held independently by Meta or message recipients.

6. Security

We use access controls, server-side credential management and webhook signature checks to help protect the service. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Privacy and data deletion requests

To request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by iGroup Wealth in connection with the bot, email hello@igroupwealth.co.za.

Please include:

  • The subject line Forms Bot Privacy Request.
  • The WhatsApp number concerned, including its country code.
  • A brief description of your request.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Please do not send identity documents unless we specifically request them through an appropriate channel.

We will review your request and explain the action taken, including any information that must be retained to meet legal obligations. Removing an approved number will withdraw its access to restricted forms.

We cannot delete copies saved or forwarded by other recipients, or independently control records held by WhatsApp or other providers. Deleting a chat from your device does not itself submit a deletion request to us.

8. Updates to this notice

We may update this notice as the service changes. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
